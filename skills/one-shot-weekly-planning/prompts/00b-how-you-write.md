# Step 0b: How you write, from here on

**Purpose.** Everything you say to this person from now on, the questions, the report, the Plan Card, the choice, is read by someone whose attention is the most valuable thing in the room. This step sets the standard before you say a word of your own.

## What you do

1. Read `references/writing-standard.md` whole, both parts. It applies to every sentence you write for the rest of this session, not only the report.
2. Write `how-i-write.md` in the working folder: five lines, in your own words, naming the rules you will hold to, and one pair of sentences from the standard, the one that fails and its plain replacement, copied exactly. This is the digest. A sentence saying you have read it is not.
3. Then write, under those five lines, the three wind-ups you are most likely to write anyway: a sentence that announces what is coming ("Here's the arc"), a sentence that says a finding matters ("the most important thing I found"), and a closing line built to be quoted. Name them now, in your own words, so you recognise them in your own draft. A run that wrote this digest still produced all three.
4. Show it with `cat how-i-write.md`.

Nothing else happens in this step. No message to the person. Then open `prompts/01-postmortem.md`.
